15 customers across 14 CSPs, taken end to end by the field team. 12 of 15 were faults in the network we run, not the television — the TV is simply where the customer notices, being the device that is on every evening. Full case-by-case report: tv-camera-rca.html.

The finding that changes how we work: the biggest single category is a 2.4 GHz-only television that keeps losing the network while the rest of the house stays online. Two of those four were fixed remotely by giving the 2.4 and 5 GHz bands separate names; the other two got the Wi-Fi box replaced, which achieved the same thing only because a new unit comes up with fresh names. Genuine dead hardware accounts for two cases, both of which showed no network name on any device. Checking the ping record before authorising a swap separates the two in seconds.

Bonus paid is what actually reached the CSP's settlement wallet (BONUS_CREDIT on the payment-settlement ledger, current to the hour). Credits post on the 1st and the 16th, so the 16-31 Aug cycle is the credit dated 1 Sep and the 1-15 Sep cycle is the credit dated 16 Sep. The analytics bonus tables were unusable - PARTNER_BONUS_DISBURSEMENT stops 16 Jun, PARTNER_INCENTIVES 23 Jun, WORK_BONUS_TXNS 1 Jun - so this reads the ledger the money moved through. 9 of these ten have had nothing credited in either cycle. Last credit dates across the ten: 01 Aug (4), 16 Aug (3), 16 Jul (2), 15 Sep (1). Across the estate the 1-15 Sep cycle has credited 288 CSPs so far against 278 for the cycle before.
