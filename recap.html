The CSPs that were failing last week did resolve far better on what they carried — 20 of 70 (28.6%) last week against 34 of 54 (63.0%) this week, +34.4 pp — but they carried 54 cases this week against 110 last week, so the lift is worth about 3.5 pp on the overall rate. Against it, the CSPs that were holding up last week moved −11.4 pp. Taken together, the CSPs present in both weeks moved the rate by −1.6 pp; the remaining +5.3 pp is composition — 239 of this week's 524 cases (45.6%) came from 168 CSPs that had nothing mature last week at all, and they resolve at 77.8%.

Of the 35 CSPs that were failing last week, 20 had a case mature again this week and 6 of those now clear 80% or better.
